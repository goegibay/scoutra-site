FAQ

Why do creators arrive in weekly batches?

Because verification takes real work. We deliver at the pace we can actually verify — you always see how many are in the queue. We don't pad lists to look big.

Where does the data come from?

Public creator profiles across TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram, combined with app-store keyword data — then verified by our own pipeline. We record the source of every contact.

What if a contact goes dead?

That's the point of monitoring: weekly snapshots catch dead emails and dormant accounts, and we flag them before you write to them.
